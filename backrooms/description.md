# Garmin Connect IQ Store Description

## Backrooms Run: Horror Escape

You noclipped out of reality and landed in the Backrooms: endless mono-yellow
rooms, damp carpet and the hum of fluorescent lights. Backrooms Run is a
pseudo-3D horror escape game with a real Wolfenstein-style raycasting engine on
your watch — textured wallpaper, ceiling tiles and carpet rendered in first
person, at speed, on a screen the size of a coin.

### How to play
- Swipe up to walk, swipe left/right to turn, swipe down to use what is in front
  of you
- LIGHT button is your torch — the beam is the only thing down here that pushes
  back
- MENU is a panic sprint, and it costs you the breath you do not have
- Reach the exit to drop a level deeper; every level is darker and hungrier

### Two clocks, both falling
- Sanity always drains, three times faster in the dark. Almond water restores it
- Torch battery burns while lit. Spare cells are rare, so the beam is a decision

### What is down there
- The Stalker watches from the end of a hall and is gone when you reach it
- The Shadow only exists in the dark. Meeting its eye holds it still and feeds
  it — the torch burns it away instead
- The Mimic wears the exit sign. You find out which one it was by touching it
- Lights die, walls genuinely move, halls grow longer, and something walks
  behind you

### Features
- Procedural floors, so no two runs are the same
- Daily seeded run: everyone gets the identical maze
- Save and resume mid-run, plus global leaderboards for longest run, deepest
  level, most escapes and daily

Short runs, high tension, one more try. Compete worldwide at bitochi.com.
