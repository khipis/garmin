# Garmin Connect IQ Store Description

## Daily Sport Challenge

One challenge. One day. Everyone on Earth. Daily Sport Challenge hands every
player the exact same skill test each morning, then ranks the whole world on
it. The sport rotates — the fairness never does.

### Six sports in the rotation
- Basketball — the jump shot, off the rim and the glass
- Football — the free kick, over a keeper into the top corner
- Archery — a flat, fast arrow at a ten-ring target face
- Tennis — the serve, between the net cord and the baseline
- Golf — the chip, judged on how close it pitches to the pin
- Hill ride — the ski jump, flying for the K-point

### How to play
- Tap once to lock your aim
- Tap again to set the power
- Tap on the beat to release — timing is everything
- Hit the day's objective before the clock runs out

### Features
- Real physics per sport: gravity, drag, bounce and travel, never a dice roll
- A live trajectory guide, so every miss tells you what to fix
- Four rotating daily objectives: sprint, perfect, streak and tight target
- Practice any sport you like, any day, for free and unranked
- Steps and active minutes become extra attempts, calories become coins
- Unlock the golden and flame balls, plus night, sunset and stadium venues
- Day streaks, personal bests and yesterday's score to beat
- Global leaderboard per sport and objective — same challenge for everyone
- Plays fully offline; scores upload when your watch syncs

Skill, not luck. Compete worldwide at bitochi.com.
