# Garmin Connect IQ Store Description

## Zombie Survival: Last Stand

Your steps are the only ammunition. Walk during the day, spend what you
scavenge on walls and turrets, and at 21:00 the horde comes down the street
whether you are watching or not.

This is not a shooter. You never move, aim or clear a wave. You prepare, and
then you find out whether it was enough.

### How to play
- Real steps and workouts pay scrap through the day
- Spend it on walls, gate, MG nest, mortar, tesla coil, traps and passive
  systems
- Watch the countdown on every screen: WAVE IN 4h 07m
- At 21:00 the wave resolves by itself — offline nights are waiting for you
  on next open
- Hold on and the next night gets bigger. Get overrun and the same night
  comes back tomorrow

### Features
- One wave per day, automatic, online or off
- A preview that tells you what is coming and how it looks for you — never
  a promise, the simulation wobbles
- Watchable nights: three lanes, seven zombie types, an Abomination every
  fifth night, your turrets firing without you
- Hold SELECT while watching to fire your own rifle — a bonus for turning up
- Eleven upgrade tracks, fifteen levels each
- No punishment wipe: a lost night costs a day, never what you built
- Global leaderboards: furthest night, strongest fort, most kills, nights held

The whole game is the hour before dark. Compete worldwide at bitochi.com.
