# Garmin Connect IQ Store Description

## Dungeon Master: Watch Crawler RPG

A 90s dungeon crawler on your wrist. Dungeon Master: Watch Crawler is a
first-person pseudo-3D RPG — explore procedurally generated floors, fight
monsters turn by turn, loot better gear and descend as deep as you dare.

### How to play
- Swipe left and right to turn, swipe up to step forward
- One button opens doors, searches walls and attacks whatever blocks the
  corridor
- Beat every fifth floor guardian and take the stairs down

### Features
- Lightweight raycasting engine with torch-lit corridors
- Turn-based combat: attack, power attack, defend or drink
- Skeletons, goblins, spiders, knights and boss demons
- Loot from Rust Sword to Crystal Blade, armor, scrolls and keys
- Level up your own way: HP, strength, defense or magic
- Secret chambers hidden behind fake walls
- Daily dungeon — the same seed for every player
- Steps become Adventure Energy for a small head start
- Save and resume your expedition
- Global leaderboard — deepest floor and richest haul
