# Garmin Connect IQ Store Description

## Tower Defense: Waves & Strategy

Place towers. Read the wave. Rebuild your layout. Tower Defense is a full
strategy game on your wrist — 30 waves, seven towers with four upgrade tiers
each, a boss every fifth wave, and an economy that pays interest if you hold
your nerve.

### How to play
- Pick a build pad beside the road and buy a tower
- Upgrade, retarget or sell between waves — the bottom sheet always names what
  SELECT will do
- Read the wave modifier before it spawns, then fix your weak side
- Survive 30 waves without letting your base HP hit zero

### Features
- Seven towers — Turret, Frost, Archer, Flame, Cannon, Tesla, Sniper — four
  tiers each, and the silhouette visibly changes as they level
- Per-tower targeting priority: first, strongest or closest
- Seven enemy types plus bosses with rotating Rage, Ward, Summon and Regen
  abilities
- Wave modifiers: Swarm, Armored, Blitz and Air Raid, each announced before it
  lands
- Airstrike and Deep Freeze abilities on cooldown
- Interest on unspent coins, so hoarding is a real strategic option
- Four maps with genuinely different shapes, plus a daily challenge shared by
  every player
- Post-wave diagnosis names the towers that carried the wave and the one thing
  to fix
- Global leaderboard — best score and deepest wave
- Save and resume mid-run, and every option persists between sessions

Smart placement beats grinding. Compete worldwide at bitochi.com.
